FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Do you take equity from all programs?▾
Only our flagship programs (Launchpad Core and Global Accelerator) take equity. IdeaLab is completely equity-free and free of charge. ScaleUp uses a revenue-share model instead.
Is the incubation in-person or online?▾
LaunchPad Core and ScaleUp have an optional co-working space in Bangalore. All other programs are fully remote-first with live online workshops. You can participate from anywhere in India.
Can I apply if I don't have a registered company yet?▾
Yes! For IdeaLab and Launchpad Core, you can apply as an individual or team. We help you incorporate during the program if needed.
What sectors do you focus on?▾
We are sector-agnostic but have particular strength in AgriTech, FinTech, HealthTech, EdTech, B2B SaaS, and DeepTech. Over 30% of our portfolio is in climate or sustainability-adjacent sectors.
When are applications open?▾
Each program has rolling admissions with 2-3 cohorts per year. Check the deadline shown on each program card above, or login to the platform to apply anytime.

🌟 Our Story

Building India's Startup Future

LaunchPad was founded on a simple belief: the best ideas deserve the best support. We built the platform we wish existed when we were founders.

Our Mission

Democratizing Access to the Startup Ecosystem

India produces over 1,000 startups every month, but only a fraction get the structured support they need to survive the first 18 months. LaunchPad exists to change that ratio.

We combine cohort-based learning, dedicated mentorship, investor access, and operational support into one integrated platform — designed specifically for Indian founders building globally relevant startups.

Our model is simple: your success is our success. We don't just provide resources. We roll up our sleeves and build alongside you.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

🤝
Founder First
Every decision we make is filtered through one question: "Is this good for the founder?" Your success is the only metric that matters to us.
🔓
Radical Transparency
We share everything — our methods, our mistakes, and our networks. No black boxes, no gatekeeping. Founders thrive when they can see clearly.
⚡
Bias for Action
Startups don't die from moving too fast; they die from moving too slow. We push our founders to build, test, and learn faster than they thought possible.
🌱
Long-term Thinking
We take a 10-year view. We care more about building sustainable businesses than chasing short-term metrics or flashy press coverage.
🧩
Ecosystem Mindset
We believe startups grow faster together. Our cohort-based model is intentional — we want founders learning from, competing with, and supporting each other.
🇮🇳
India-First Ambition
We are proud to be building from India, for the world. We understand the unique advantages and challenges of the Indian market deeply.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

🌱
Jan 2022
The Idea
Founded by two ex-founders frustrated with the fragmented support system for Indian startups. Built the first version of the platform in 6 weeks.
🚀
June 2022
First Cohort
Launched Cohort 2022-A with 8 startups. All 8 survived the program; 5 went on to raise external funding within 12 months.
📈
2023
Rapid Expansion
Grew to 4 cohorts and 50+ mentors. Launched the ScaleUp program. Portfolio startups crossed ₹5Cr in combined annual revenue.
🌍
2024
Global Reach
Launched the Global Accelerator with desk access in Singapore and San Francisco. First international Demo Day with 120+ investors attending.
⚡
2025 — Today
Platform Era
Launched this full-stack incubation platform connecting 120+ startups, 50+ mentors, and 80+ investors in one integrated ecosystem.
